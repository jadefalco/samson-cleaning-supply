Our Story

About Samson Cleaning Supply

A Family Commitment to Quality

Ken and Gale-Paule Davison have called Salmon Arm home for over two decades. In that time, they have built Samson Cleaning Supply into a trusted local resource for professional cleaning products, equipment, and expertise.

What makes us different? We are not a faceless distributor. We are a family business that knows its customers by name. We understand the unique needs of hotels, restaurants, schools, and farms in the Shuswap because we live here too.

Our flagship line, Samson Soap, has been designed, formulated, and manufactured in Kelowna, BC for over twenty five years. These are not imported chemicals with a local label — they are genuinely BC-made products built for Western Canadian conditions.
