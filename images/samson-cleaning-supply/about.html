Our Story

For over 20 years, Samson Cleaning Supply has been Salmon Arm's trusted partner for professional cleaning supplies, vacuum repair, and eco-friendly products.

Built on Trust, Run with Heart

Samson Cleaning Supply started with a simple mission: provide Salmon Arm with high-quality cleaning products and genuine, personal service. What began as a small family operation has grown into a cornerstone of the local business community.

Over the past two decades, we've supplied everything from household detergents to industrial floor scrubbers. We've helped hotels prepare for peak season, restaurants maintain health standards, schools keep classrooms safe, and homeowners keep their spaces spotless.

"When you shop local, you invest in your community. Every delivery we make, every repair we complete, every recommendation we give — it's about keeping Salmon Arm clean, healthy, and thriving."

— Ken & Gale-Paule Davison

Our Values

These aren't just words on a wall — they're the principles that guide every decision we make, every product we stock, and every customer we serve.

  • Community First — We live here, work here, and raise our families here. Salmon Arm's success is our success.
  • Environmental Responsibility — We prioritize eco-friendly, refillable, and Canadian-made products.
  • Honest Service — We'll fix your vacuum before we sell you a new one. We recommend the right product, not the most expensive one.
  • Reliability — When you need supplies, you need them now. We keep our shelves stocked and our delivery routes running.
